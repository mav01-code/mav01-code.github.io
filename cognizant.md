## Experience

### Interview

Interview is on Superset platform. You have to be online and keep refreshing till you get a call. They will assign you a panel. I had 2 members for mine. Both are seated in their respective seats (off-site at college). For some, 1 member was online and other was offline. Interview started with my introduction, project explanation, internship experience (whatever I mentioned on my resume). They asked core Java questions - explain JVM, JRE, JDK; OOP concepts. A lot of React questions - component composition, useState & useReference, state, props, can we mutate a state, DOM, virtual DOM. Asked me to rate myself in Java - my overconfident ass said 4/5 and they started giving me looks now. Damn, I thought they were gonna give me some DP or graph question but they ended up asking me code factorial, find duplicates in a list, read an excel file using Java. They asked me if I know anything about Spring Boot, for which I answered a bit and I mostly use FastAPI and they didn't ask me any further questions.
