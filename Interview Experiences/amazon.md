## Experience

### OA - Online Assessment

It contains 2 coding questions and 1 workstyle assessment. There is no camera during this round. I completed 1 coding question completely and 2nd one partially. Workstyle assessment is just similar to behavioral.

### Interviews

In the first round I was asked to explain my projects and a few gen ai questions like how I used gen ai in my projects and which tool, which version was it. A few behavioral questions like is there a time when you dug deep to identify root cause analysis?

Then we moved to the coding round. I was asked a question on dfs like there's a grid that contains 0, 1 and 2. 0 signifies empty, 1 fresh and 2 means spoiled. Within a minute all the neighboring 1's of 2 will become spoiled. Calculate the minimum number of minutes that take to spoil the complete bay area(grid). It took me around 30 mins to come to the solution and explain it.

In the second round, again introduce yourself and explain about your projects. Then another LP question like is there a time when you had to deliver a project under strict deadline and deliver it. And another one is explain when you made a mistake and acknowledged it. Later we moved to the coding part where I was asked a question like there's a list of strings and find a unique prefix for each. l = [dog, duck, dove, xyz, zebra] dog - dog, duck - du, dove - dov, xyz - x, zebra - z. It took me some significant amount of time to reach brute force after multiple hints and I couldn't optimizer this further. We have to use a tree to find the unique prefix for each (Trie).

That's about it. Both the interviewers are very friendly and gave me a lot of time to think about a solution. Second one even ended up giving a lot of hints since I was stuck.
